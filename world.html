🏆 ICC Cricket World Cup

The ICC Cricket World Cup is the biggest tournament in One Day International cricket, held every four years and contested by the world's best teams.

🌟 World Cup Facts

  • 🏆 Australia has won the most Cricket World Cup titles.
  • 🇮🇳 India won the World Cup in 1983 and 2011.
  • 👑 Sachin Tendulkar scored the most runs in World Cup history.
  • 🎯 Glenn McGrath took the most wickets in World Cup history.
  • 🌍 The first Cricket World Cup was played in 1975.
